# Fundamentals of Drones and UAV Systems

## Overview

This repository contains my assignment submission for the **Summer School 2026 – IoT & Drones Program** conducted by **Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Jammu**.

The assignment focuses on the fundamentals of drones (UAVs), their components, flight principles, sensors, navigation systems, control mechanisms, and real-world applications. It also includes higher-order thinking questions, labeled diagrams, and practical activities to strengthen the understanding of drone technology.

---

## Topics Covered

- Introduction to UAVs and their evolution
- Real-world applications of drones
- Types of drones and UAV systems
- Quadcopter flight principles
- Drone movements and flight dynamics
- Drone components and their functions
- Sensors used in drones
- Sensor comparisons
- Six Degrees of Freedom (6DOF)
- Signal flow in drones
- PID control
- Obstacle avoidance
- Return-to-Home (RTH)
- Practical drone component identification
- Higher-order thinking questions
